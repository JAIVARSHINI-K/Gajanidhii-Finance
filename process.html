Our Process

1

Submission & Examination

  • Original RC (If needed TO set & NOC)
  • Vehicle Examination
2

Borrower & Guarantor Details

  • Address, Occupation Details etc
3

Loan Amount Quoted & Approval

  • Based on vehicle condition, fine amount, vehicle model, ownership , registration.,etc loan & EMI amount will be quoted
4

Documentation

  • If approved by customer, documentation will be done
5

Verification

  • FI will be initiated to Borrower House & if needed also to Guarantor House (on the willing of the Borrower)
6

Loan Sanction

  • If FI seems to be okay, loan amount will be sanctioned
7

Payment

  • Payment will be released either by Cash or through Bank (A/c provided by the customer) at the same day or next day (Based on the time of loan sanction)
